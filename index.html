University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign

Interactive Computing

Human-computer interaction at Illinois spans the Siebel School and partner units across campus. The group designs and studies systems for social computing, creativity, visualization, immersive environments, health, privacy, and learning.

Faculty and students publish at CHI, CSCW, UIST, and related HCI venues, as well as ACL, EMNLP, NAACL, and related NLP venues. The community meets roughly weekly for the HCI Seminar.
