Nine papers from Interactive Computing were accepted to EMNLP 2026, six in the main conference and three in Findings. Together they cover online communities and social media, human–AI interaction, and health and wellbeing.

They are also listed on the [publications page]({{ '/publications/' | relative_url }}).

## Main conference

- [The Hidden Toll of Social Media News on Psychosocial Wellbeing](https://aclanthology.org/2026.emnlp-main.5/). Olivia Pal, Agam Goyal, Eshwar Chandrasekharan, and Koustuv Saha.
- [VASTU: Language Models Struggle to Recognize Online Community Values](https://aclanthology.org/2026.emnlp-main.231/). Agam Goyal, Xianyang Zhan, Charlotte Lambert, Koustuv Saha, and Eshwar Chandrasekharan.
- [AI Psychosis: Does Conversational AI Amplify Delusion-Related Language?](https://aclanthology.org/2026.emnlp-main.20/). Soorya Ram Shimgekar, Vipin Gunda, Jiwon Kim, Violeta J. Rodriguez, Hari Sundaram, and Koustuv Saha.
- [LLUMI: Improving LLM Writing Assistance for Mental Health Support with Online Community Feedback](https://aclanthology.org/2026.emnlp-main.2254/). Jiwon Kim, Maya Ajit, Sherry Gong, Soorya Ram Shimgekar, Dong Whi Yoo, Eshwar Chandrasekharan, and Koustuv Saha.
- [FOXGLOVE: Understanding Goal-Oriented and Anchored Writing Feedback from Experts and LLMs on Argumentative Essays](https://aclanthology.org/2026.emnlp-main.781/). Yijun Liu, Yifan Song, John R. Gallagher, Sarah Sterman, and Tal August.
- [Evaluating the Hidden Costs of Personalization in Large Language Models](https://aclanthology.org/2026.emnlp-main.679/). Yumeng Wang, Yuchen Wu, Cheng Qian, Zhiyuan Fan, Hyeonjeong Ha, Shujin Wu, Jiayu Liu, Heng Ji, and Ge Wang.

## Findings

- [Answer Bubbles: Information Exposure in AI-Mediated Search](https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-emnlp.8/). Michelle Huang, Agam Goyal, Koustuv Saha, and Eshwar Chandrasekharan.
- [Facts Without Rules: Boundary Metadata Collapse in Multi-Agent LLM Handoffs](https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-emnlp.422/). Yian Wang, Agam Goyal, Eshwar Chandrasekharan, and Hari Sundaram.
- [TUX: Measuring Human–AI Tacit Understanding](https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-emnlp.1090/). Yueshen Li, Hanyi Min, Vedant Das Swain, and Koustuv Saha.
