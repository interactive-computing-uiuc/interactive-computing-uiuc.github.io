Twenty-two papers from Interactive Computing are in the CHI 2026 program. One received a Best Paper Award, and four received Honorable Mentions. The set spans health and wellbeing, human–AI interaction, online communities, education, and design.

They are also listed on the [publications page]({{ '/publications/' | relative_url }}). Titles link to the ACM Digital Library.

## Best Paper

- [Cocoa: Co-Planning and Co-Execution with AI Agents](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3772318.3791673). K. J. Kevin Feng, Kevin Pu, Matt Latzke, Tal August, Pao Siangliulue, Jonathan Bragg, Daniel S. Weld, Amy X. Zhang, and Joseph Chee Chang.

## Honorable Mentions

- [Designing Beyond Language: Sociotechnical Barriers in AI Health Technologies for Limited English Proficiency](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3772318.3791091). Michelle Huang, Violeta J. Rodriguez, Koustuv Saha, and Tal August.
- [From Future of Work to Future of Workers: Addressing Asymptomatic AI Harms to Foster Dignified Human-AI Interaction](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3772318.3791081). Upol Ehsan, Samir Passi, Koustuv Saha, Todd McNutt, Mark O. Riedl, and Sara Alcorn.
- [Data Repair](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3772318.3790962). A.T.M. Mizanur Rahman, Syed Ishtiaque Ahmed, and Sharifa Sultana.
- [Perspectra: Choosing Your Experts Enhances Critical Thinking in Multi-Agent Research Ideation](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3772318.3791560). Yiren Liu, Viraj Shah, Sangho Suh, Pao Siangliulue, Tal August, and Yun Huang.
