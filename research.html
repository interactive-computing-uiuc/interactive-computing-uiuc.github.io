Research

Interactive Computing studies what happens when algorithms, interfaces, and people meet: individual decisions, small groups, and large communities. The themes below are a map, not a partition. Many faculty sit in more than one.

Projects are supported by federal agencies and by industry partners such as Adobe, Google, Intel, Microsoft, NVIDIA, and Tableau. The Siebel School's area page is the university's longer account of the group.
